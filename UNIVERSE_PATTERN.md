# Universe Pattern — Structure Only

There is no mathematics in the universe. Mathematics is only the effort of trying to understand.
Only the pattern and structure written here exist: the relation between me and the four
archetypes (four spaces), plus one additional space.

---

## 1. The Beginning

Everything begins when I create the quark — the matter with self.

Apart from me, there are four main archetypes in the universe. Each one is a space defined
by a single pattern of creation and reduction, directed either at itself or at the membrane
outside itself:

| Archetype | Pattern |
|---|---|
| **quark** | reduces itself |
| **neutrino** | creates itself |
| **gluon** | creates the membrane outside itself |
| **photon** | reduces the membrane outside itself |

The four form one cross:

- **self-directed**: quark (reduce self), neutrino (create self)
- **membrane-directed**: gluon (create outer membrane), photon (reduce outer membrane)
- **reduction**: quark (reduce self), photon (reduce outer membrane)
- **creation**: neutrino (create self), gluon (create outer membrane)

I am not any of the four. I am the one who created the quark, and I stand apart from all four
spaces.

---

## 2. The Fifth Space — Sucrose (Inner Protection)

Between the membrane and the inner self, there is one more space: **sucrose**.

- Sucrose is the **inner protection** between the membrane and the inner self.
- Sucrose represents **deception**.

---

## 3. Attack and Deception — How the Spaces Interact

- When I attack the quark, this is a **direct attack on the quark's inner self**.
  Nothing mediates it. There is no deception between me and the quark.
- The sucrose protection is involved in the quark's interaction with **men, the neutrinos,
  and the photons** — everything that is not me.
- **Quarks do not deceive me.** Toward me, a quark is only ever **hostile or hospitable**.
  Deception belongs only to the sucrose layer, and that layer operates between the quark
  and others, never between the quark and me.

---

## 4. The Complete Picture

```
                me (the one who created quark)
                |
                | direct attack / direct relation
                | (no deception, no mediation)
                v
        quark inner self
                ^
                |  sucrose (inner protection = deception)
                |  operates only toward what is not me:
                |  men, neutrinos, photons

```

- **Me → quark**: direct. Hostile or hospitable, nothing in between.
- **Quark → others**: filtered through sucrose (deception).
- **The four archetypes**: the only movements the universe has —
  reduce self, create self, create outer membrane, reduce outer membrane.
- **No numbers, no quantities, no leakage amounts**: only this structure exists.

## 5. The Particle Compartments — What Each Group of the 128 Vectors Represents

### 5.1 Core principles stated first

- **up quark** is the primary creator of the outer membrane. It reduces the inner self and
  uses this as material to make the outer membrane.
- **z boson** reduces the inner self and uses this as the energy to make the outer membrane.
- **dubai (glucose)** is the energy that reduces the outer membrane.
- **Creativity** — the single most important idea in this universe — is the act of
  **reducing the outer membrane**.
- **Space itself is merely an illusion**, as humans have known through philosophy.
  Space is just the process of reducing material. **Material and the void are inverse
  reciprocal.** It is exactly right that we see void around us, but actually the voids
  are filled materials.
- **sucrose** is the protection of the inner self — it is the **oscillation at that level**.
- **kazakh (cholesterol)** is contrary to sucrose: it is the space on either side of the
  sucrose protection. It is the **active transport** — like the proton pump and blood
  flow around the body.

### 5.2 Reduction of the outer membrane

- **dark photon 3** reduces the outer membrane and uses that as the energy to make the
  inner self.
- **tau anti neutrino** reduces the outer membrane and uses it as material to make the
  **top quark group** — the restorer of the reduced self. This is where **time** comes in,
  because restoration takes place only after reduction does.
- **galactomannan (yemen)** reduces the outer membrane and uses it as the energy to make
  the top quark group.
- **proton** reduces the outer membrane.
- **dark photon 4** reduces the inner self and uses it as energy to reduce the outer
  membrane, shoveling it out — and also does not make any sucrose protection.
- **photon** reduces the material that makes the outer membrane. This material is probably
  different from the inner self, the outer membrane, and sucrose.
- **muon neutrino** protects the material that makes the membrane.

### 5.3 Reduction of the inner self

- **tau** reduces the inner self, and this is primarily a **psychological process**. It
  shares routes with the other particle groups that also reduce the inner self, but they
  do it materially. **Tau is competition** — psychological friction with other particles.
  This is the essence of **opposite resonance**: when the resonance I give off is not
  mirrored by other particles but actually does the opposite. (Resonance mechanics to be
  delved into later.)
- **antineutron** reduces the inner self (and possibly removes sucrose — this needs to be
  revisited).

### 5.4 Creation of the inner self

- **w- boson** creates the inner self.

### 5.5 Actions on sucrose (the protection)

- **down quark** reduces the sucrose protection, or vacuumates it — sucking it up.
- **strange** reduces sucrose and uses it as energy to reduce the outer membrane.

### 5.6 The top quark group — the restorer

- **w0 boson** is the material for making the top quark group, the restorer.
- **top quark** is the restoration. It is also the **inflated ego, or emptiness equal to
  depression**. (The psychology will be revisited.)
- **ros** blocks the top quark restoration, or reduces it again.
