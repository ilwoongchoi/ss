# Structure + Wave Unification
## The Continuous Wave Field and Its Discrete Structural Skeleton

---

## The Unification Thesis

### Primary Principle

**The seismic wave field is continuous**. The Earth medium propagates elastic energy from source to receiver through every point along the path. There is no "gap" in the wave field itself—only gaps in our observation of it.

### Emergent Principle

**Mapped geological structure is discrete**. Faults, layer boundaries, and tectonic blocks emerge from analyzing wave-field discontinuities, arrival patterns, and impedance contrasts. Structure is the skeleton extracted from the wave body.

### Closure-Grammar Principle

**Apparent wave-field discontinuities are projection traps**. True barriers (impedance boundaries) exist, but many "gaps" in seismic data are artifacts of sparse station coverage, phase identification thresholds, or oversimplified reference models. Closure grammar reconstructs wave-field continuity using minimal mediator phases.

## The Wave-Structure Duality

### Fault as Wave-Field Feature

A mapped fault is **not** a line on the ground—it is:
- A zone of scattering in the wave field
- A barrier to some phases (reflected waves)
- A guide for others (fault-zone trapped waves)
- A converter of phases (P-to-S at fault interface)

### Gap as Observation Artifact

A gap in fault mapping is **not** necessarily a break in structure—it is:
- A region where wave-field expression is subtle
- A projection trap requiring mediator phases
- A continuity recoverable via converted/scattered arrivals

### Wave as Primary Carrier

The seismic wave **knows** the structure:
- Travel times encode velocity structure
- Amplitudes encode attenuation (Q)
- Conversions encode impedance boundaries
- Scattering encodes heterogeneity spectrum
