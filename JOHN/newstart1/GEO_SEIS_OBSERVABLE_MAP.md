# Geology/Seismology Observable Map

## Observable: FAULT SEGMENT (Node)

**Closure mapping**: Node

**Measurement**:
- Endpoint coordinates (lat, lon, elevation)
- Strike and dip
- Sense of motion (normal, reverse, strike-slip)
- Cumulative displacement

**Component role**: Primary node in DSU

**Closure condition**: Segment qualifies as node when kinematically consistent with adjacent segments

---

## Observable: SEGMENT ALIGNMENT (Bridge Contact)

**Closure mapping**: Bridge weight / Contact metric

**Measurement**:
- Strike alignment (±15° tolerance)
- Projected distance across gap
- Elevation profile consistency
- Step-over geometry (right/left lateral)

**Component role**: Determines bridge viability

**Closure condition**:
- High alignment + short gap → strong contact (bridge likely valid)
- Poor alignment + long gap → weak contact (projection trap or barrier)

---

## Observable: KINEMATIC CONSISTENCY (Bridge Saddle)

**Closure mapping**: Saddle metric / Geometric compatibility

**Measurement**:
- Slip sense consistency (all normal, all reverse, etc.)
- Strain rate compatibility
- Focal mechanism similarity (for seismic segments)
- Stress field orientation

**Component role**: Big Woman (shell veto) — kinematic inconsistency breaks bridge

**Closure condition**: Compatible stress/strain regime across gap

---

## Observable: SEISMIC CORRIDOR (Latent Mediator)

**Closure mapping**: External mediator

**Measurement**:
- Microseismicity alignment beneath gap
- Seismic reflection structure
- Seismic velocity anomaly
- InSAR line-of-sight displacement gradient

**Component role**: Extended universe node enabling 2→1 closure

**Closure condition**: Mediator must connect two otherwise disconnected fault segments

**Falsifiable signature**:
- Linear microseismicity beneath surface gap
- Seismic velocity contrast aligned with fault trend
- InSAR gradient continuous across surface gap

---

## Observable: SURFACE GAP (Projection Trap)

**Closure mapping**: Barrier vs. Projection trap distinction

**Measurement**:
- Gap length along strike
- Cover type (alluvium, vegetation, water)
- Erosion depth
- Drainage displacement across gap

**Component role**: Internal boundary where surface observation fails

**Critical distinction**:
- Standard view: Gap = "fault terminates"
- Closure view: Gap = "projection trap — mediator may exist"

---

## Observable: CROSS-CUTTING RELATIONSHIP (True Barrier)

**Closure mapping**: True barrier / A↔C uncrossable

**Measurement**:
- Fault A displaced by Fault B
- Opposing kinematics (normal vs. reverse)
- Age relationship (A older than B, cutting A)
- Different structural styles

**Component role**: Hard veto on bridge formation

**Closure condition**: No mediator can cross true barrier

---

## Observable: MULTI-SEGMENT CHAIN (Relay)

**Closure mapping**: Lane C relay

**Measurement**:
- Three or more segments with consistent trend
- Intermediate segment spans gap region
- Cumulative displacement matches total offset

**Component role**: Indirect connection via intermediate structure

**Closure condition**: Relay segment kinematically compatible with both endpoints

---

## Observable: SUBSURFACE STRUCTURE (Mediator Evidence)

**Closure mapping**: Hidden lift / Deprojection

**Measurement**:
- Depth to basement from seismic reflection
- Fault plane solution alignment
- Bouguer gravity anomaly gradient
- Aeromagnetic lineament

**Component role**: Reveals hidden continuity beneath projection trap

---

## Observable: GEOMORPHIC EXPRESSION (Small Man)

**Closure mapping**: Local seam substrate

**Measurement**:
- Linear valleys / ridges
- Offset drainage patterns
- Spring alignment
- Vegetation lineaments

**Component role**: Direct segment-to-segment connection without mediator

---

## Observable: MISSING SURFACE EXPRESSION (Small Woman)

**Closure mapping**: Projection trap

**Measurement**:
- Alluvial cover thickness
- Lack of fault scarp in expected position
- Undisplaced Quaternary deposits
- Absence of microseismicity at surface projection

**Component role**: Local evidence insufficient; deprojection or external mediator required
