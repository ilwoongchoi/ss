# Seismic-Wave Closure Grammar
## Wave as Continuous Carrier, Fault as Discrete Skeleton

---

## Core Reframing

**Standard view**: Seismology studies earthquakes and faults; waves are merely the detection mechanism.

**Closure-grammar view**: **Seismic waves are the continuous carrier of connectivity**. Fault segments are the discrete skeleton that emerges from wave-field discontinuities. The wave field is primary; mapped structure is secondary.

### The Unification Thesis

> **Seismic waves propagate continuously through the Earth medium. Apparent discontinuities in wave arrival patterns are not always true impedance breaks, but often projection traps caused by sparse station coverage, phase identification thresholds, or buried structural complexity. Closure grammar reconstructs wave-field continuity beyond standard phase association by introducing minimal mediator phases (converted waves, scattered arrivals, low-amplitude corridors), while true impedance barriers remain uncrossable.**

---

## Wave-Centric Element Definitions

### WAVE FIELD (The Continuous Carrier)

**Definition**: The complete elastic displacement field u(x,t) propagating through the Earth medium.

**Properties**:
- Continuous everywhere except at true impedance boundaries
- Carries information about medium properties along entire path
- Observable only at discrete station locations (projection)

**Role**: The underlying reality that closure grammar seeks to reconstruct.

---

### NODE (Discrete Wave Observation)

**Definition**: A detected wave arrival with measurable properties.

**Types**:
- **Pick**: Phase arrival time at station (P, S, surface wave)
- **Hypocenter**: Inferred source location from arrival pattern
- **Structural endpoint**: Where wave field shows observable discontinuity

**Closure role**: Discrete sample of continuous wave field; entry point into DSU.

---

### BRIDGE (Phase-Consistent Connection)

**Definition**: Hypothesis linking two nodes via continuous wave propagation.

**Properties**:
- **Contact**: Amplitude/frequency coherence between arrivals
- **Saddle**: Travel-time residual consistency (observed vs. predicted)
- **Phase type**: P-to-P, S-to-S, or converted (P-to-S, S-to-P)

**Closure role**: Direct wave-path hypothesis between observations.

---

### RELAY (Multi-Station / Multi-Phase Propagation Path)

**Definition**: Wave path through intermediate stations or phase conversions.

**Types**:
- **Station relay**: Source → Station A → Station B (indirect path)
- **Phase relay**: P wave → converted S → surface wave
- **Scattering relay**: Source → scatterer → receiver

**Closure role**: Indirect connection when direct phase unavailable.

---

### MEDIATOR (Converted / Scattered / Low-Amplitude Phase)

**Definition**: Minimal wave phase enabling 2→1 closure across apparent gap.

**Types**:
- **Converted phase**: P-to-S or S-to-P at impedance boundary
- **Low-amplitude corridor**: Weak but coherent arrival in shadow zone
- **Scattered wave packet**: Coda energy linking source and receiver
- **Microseismic correlation**: Ambient noise correlation revealing structure

**Closure role**: External mediator added to wave-field universe to bridge gaps.

---

### BARRIER (True Impedance Boundary)

**Definition**: Physical discontinuity where wave propagation is fundamentally altered.

**Types**:
- **Moho**: Crust-mantle boundary with impedance contrast
- **Subduction slab**: High-velocity barrier to direct phases
- **Fluid body**: S-wave shadow zone (true S-barrier)
- **Tectonic discontinuity**: Major fault with offset stratigraphy

**Closure role**: Uncrossable boundary; wave field genuinely discontinuous.

---

### PROJECTION TRAP (Missing Phase / Station Gap)

**Definition**: Apparent wave discontinuity caused by observation limitations, not true barriers.

**Types**:
- **Station gap**: No receiver in wave path region
- **Thresholded pick**: Phase below identification amplitude
- **Buried structure shadow**: Complex 3D structure obscuring simple ray path
- **Phase misidentification**: Arrival assigned wrong phase code

**Closure role**: Local evidence insufficient; mediator phase may reveal continuity.

---

### HIDDEN LIFT (Continuity in Transformed Domain)

**Definition**: Wave continuity revealed by coordinate transformation or filtering.

**Types**:
- **Travel-time residual space**: Delay pattern reveals continuous structure
- **Frequency domain**: Dispersion curve continuity across gap
- **Polarization space**: Particle motion consistency across stations
- **Receiver function**: P-to-S conversion traces continuous layering

**Closure role**: Deprojection revealing wave-field continuity invisible in raw arrivals.

---

## Phase-Continuity Theorem (Seismic)

### Internal Closure Theorem (Standard Seismology)

**Statement**: Using only standard phase picks (P, S, surface waves) at available stations, wave-field reconstruction achieves a hard bound of **N components** (disconnected arrival clusters).

**Barrier**: Station gaps and phase identification thresholds create projection traps where wave continuity cannot be established.

### Extended Closure Theorem (Wave-Mediated)

**Statement**: Adding minimal mediator phases (converted arrivals, scattered coda, ambient noise correlations) enables **N→1 reduction**, recovering wave-field continuity across apparent gaps.

**Mechanism**: Each mediator phase is a minimal external addition to the wave-field universe that bridges previously disconnected arrival clusters.
