### SKYRMION PHYSICS CLARIFICATION

**Topological Skyrmion vs Magnetic Skyrmion:**
- **Magnetic skyrmion** = physical realization in real magnetic materials (spin vortex with nonzero topological winding number, observed in experiments)
- **Topological skyrmion** = general mathematical concept describing ANY field configuration with nonzero topological winding number (can exist in magnetism, liquid crystals, Bose-Einstein condensates, etc.)
- **Relationship:** Every magnetic skyrmion IS topological, but "topological" emphasizes mathematical generality beyond physical magnetism
- **For this system:** La(57) = **topological skyrmion** (mathematical generality, applicable to neutral multi-particle coherent binding at NODE 1)
