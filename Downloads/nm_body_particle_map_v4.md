### 18.5 The Cascade Principle

Each order multiplies the previous:
- 1st order: 36 reactions → 60+ substances
- 2nd order: ~200 reactions → 125+ substances
- 3rd order: ~600 reactions → 300+ substances
- 4th order: ~1000+ reactions → 500+ substances
- ...
- n-th order → approaches the full biochemical space (~100,000+)

**The 8-particle mapping is generative** — it doesn't list every substance, it generates them through cascading reactions. Each substance is the product of a specific particle interaction chain, and the chain depth determines the complexity of the derived substance.

**This is how 8 particles explain all of biochemistry: not by enumeration, but by generative cascade.**
