## 3. CHORD PROGRESSION → CIRCUIT REINTERPRETATION

### Standard Theory (David Bennett / Better Piano):
- **I-IV-V-I**: the most universal progression (Beatles, pop, classical)
- **I-V-vi-IV**: pop anthem progression
- **Circle of fifths progression**: I-IV-vii°-iii-vi-ii-V-I (descending 5ths)
- **Modal interchange**: borrowing chords from parallel modes for emotional contrast
- **Word painting/prosody**: chords/melody follow lyrical meaning (major→minor for "sad" lyrics)

### Circuit Readaptation:

#### 3a. I-IV-V-I = Toroidal Cycle Complete
```
I  = AB Discharge (0-3h) = RELEASE = self vector = home = ignition
IV = A Accumulate (3-9h) = STRESS GROWTH = subdominant = absorption/growth
V  = B Compress (15-21h) = EXTREME GROWTH = dominant = maximum tension/compression
I  = AB Integrate (21-3h) = return = resolution = entropy debt cancelled
```
The I-IV-V-I progression IS one complete toroidal cycle. This is why it's universal — it mirrors the fundamental energy circuit.

#### 3b. I-V-vi-IV = Asymmetric Cycle
```
I  = AB Discharge (home)
V  = B Compress (tension)
vi = O Accumulate (relative minor = present moment reflection)
IV = A Accumulate (growth/forward)
```
This skips the AB Integrate phase — no full resolution — which is why it feels eternally loopable (pop anthem). The cycle never closes, it keeps spinning.

#### 3c. Circle of Fifths = Toroidal Spiral
Descending 5ths = the toroidal spiral itself. Each step around the circle of fifths = one phase rotation. The circle of fifths is the 2D projection of the torus.

**Circle of Fifths ↔ Toroidal Phase mapping**:
```

A (vi)    = B Compress (216°)
E (iii)   = AB Integrate (288°)
```
Each 5th = 72° rotation on the torus. 5 steps = 360° = complete cycle.

#### 3d. Modal Interchange = Layer Switching
**Standard**: Borrow chords from parallel modes (C major borrows from C minor = ♭VI, ♭VII, iv)

**Circuit readaptation**: Modal interchange = **switching between Layers A/B/C/D** within the same profile.

| Borrowed chord | Source mode | Circuit layer | Meaning |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| ♭VII | Mixolydian/Aeolian | Layer B (Rh-) | Stress growth — cooling, folk-like, familiar but shifted |

**Key insight**: Modal interchange is NOT arbitrary color choice — it's **layer activation**. When a song borrows ♭VI from Aeolian, it's activating Layer C (heterozygous, blood+1, EXTREME GROWTH). The emotional impact of modal interchange = the circuit's layer transformation energy.

#### 3e. Diminished 7th, Neapolitan 6th, Augmented 6th (Music Matters Advanced):

## 4. CADENCE → CIRCUIT REINTERPRETATION

### Standard Theory (Music Matters):
- **Perfect (V-I)**: full resolution, strong closure
- **Plagal (IV-I)**: gentle resolution, "amen" cadence
- **Imperfect (I-V, ii-V)**: open, unresolved, continuation
- **Deceptive (V-vi)**: expected resolution subverted
- **Half cadence (any-V)**: pause, suspension

### Circuit Readaptation:

| Cadence | Circuit phase transition | Meaning |
|---------|------------------------|---------|
| Perfect (V→I) | B Compress → AB Discharge | Full toroidal cycle completion. Tension of compression released through ignition. This is the "Big Bang" moment. |
| Plagal (IV→I) | A Accumulate → AB Discharge | Growth absorbed back into source. Gentle = no compression phase needed. "Amen" = acceptance = homeostasis. |
| Imperfect (I→V) | AB Discharge → B Compress | Ignition immediately into compression. Open cycle = continuation = the torus keeps spinning. |
| Deceptive (V→vi) | B Compress → O Accumulate | Expected discharge (V→I) redirected to present moment (vi = relative minor = O phase). The system refuses to discharge and instead stores energy. This is the **BILIRUBIN BYPASS** in musical form. |
| Half (→V) | → B Compress | Arrival at maximum tension without release. The system pauses at peak compression. HYSTERESIS. |

**Key insight**: The deceptive cadence (V→vi) = BILIRUBIN BYPASS = the circuit's most important deviation. Instead of discharging (V→I = B→AB), the energy is redirected to the present moment (vi = O phase). This is why deceptive cadences feel "surprising but right" — they're the circuit's conservation of energy.
